01 · The mark

A nib writing the fix

  • 1

    The nib

    A steel fountain-pen nib at 38°, the angle a hand holds it. Flat base, sides that curve in to a sharp tip: it says "writing" before it says "software".

  • 2

    Two facets

    One half sits slightly darker, the way polished steel catches light. It gives the mark depth without a gradient on the glyph itself.

  • 3

    Slit and breather hole

    Cut clean through, with the line across the base where the nib meets the pen. Real nib details, kept at a weight that survives 32 px.

  • 4

    The squiggle

    The wavy line Inkwell draws under a mistake, in Correction Coral. The nib is still drawing it, so the line ends at the tip.

06 · Use with care

Don't change the mark

Don't recolor

The icon is always blue with a coral squiggle.

Don't straighten or rotate

The 38° angle is part of the drawing.

Don't stretch

Scale evenly. Keep clear space of ⅛ the icon's width.
